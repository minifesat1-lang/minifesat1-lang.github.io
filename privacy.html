Classbound: Pixel Roguelike – Privacy Policy

App: Classbound: Pixel Roguelike (com.bigbatstudio.classbound)
Developer: BigBat Studio
Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Classbound does not require account registration and does not ask for your real name, email, address or phone number. Your game progress (levels, gold, gems, gear, quests, settings) is stored locally on your device. To support cross-device Cloud Save backups and global Leaderboards, the game stores an anonymous installation ID, a 10-character Cloud Save transfer code, your progress snapshot, chosen player nickname, gear score, trophy count and selected hero metadata in Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database).

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and usage/diagnostic data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – to show rewarded and interstitial ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a Google UMP consent form is shown before personalized ads; you can update your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses partner data
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Realtime Database) – for anonymous Cloud Save backups and in-game Leaderboards. Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play – for app delivery and optional in-app purchases. Payment details are processed solely by Google Play, never by us.

You can reset your advertising ID or limit personalized ads in your Android device settings at any time.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Local saves are removed if you uninstall the game or reset your progress. To request deletion of Cloud Save or Leaderboard data stored under your anonymous ID, contact us by email.

4. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.
