Monster Motel - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Monster Motel ("the Game") is developed by BigBat Studio. This policy explains what information is used when you play the Game.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or location. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app usage data. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
