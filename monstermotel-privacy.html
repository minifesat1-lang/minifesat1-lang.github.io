Monster Motel - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Monster Motel ("the Game") is developed by BigBat Studio. This policy explains what information is used when you play the Game.

Information we collect

We do not ask for or require account registration, your real name, email address or phone number. Your game progress (motel floors, upgrades, coins, gems, discovered monsters, settings) is stored on your device. If Cloud Save is active, an anonymous installation ID, a 10-character Cloud Save transfer code, and your progress snapshot are stored in Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database) so you can restore your motel on another device.

Third-party services & Advertising

The Game uses the following third-party services under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – to show rewarded and interstitial ads. Google may process device/advertising IDs, IP address (approximate location), device model and app interaction data. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown (adjustable in Settings). Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses partner data
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Realtime Database) – for anonymous Cloud Save backups and cross-device transfers. Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play – to distribute the Game and process optional in-app purchases (such as gem packs or ad removal). Payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play, never by us.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have any questions or data deletion requests, contact us at minifesat12@gmail.com.
