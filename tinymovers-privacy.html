Tiny Movers – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Tiny Movers ("the game") is developed by BigBat Studio. This policy explains what information is processed when you play.

Information we collect

BigBat Studio does not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, address or phone number. Your game progress (levels, coins, purchased truck styles, settings) is stored only on your device.

Third-party services

The game uses the following services, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – to show ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play – to deliver the game and, if offered, to process in-app purchases. Payment details are handled by Google, never by us.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

In-app purchases

The game may offer optional purchases (such as coin packs or ad removal). Any purchase is processed by Google Play.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
